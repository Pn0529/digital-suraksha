## Slide 2 – Introduction

- Cybercrime and digital scam awareness are growing concerns in the community.
- Many citizens, especially beginners and senior citizens, are vulnerable to UPI fraud, phishing, fake links, and impersonation calls.
- The project addresses this gap through a bilingual civic-tech awareness portal.
- Project context:
  - Digital payments are increasing rapidly.
  - Public awareness is often fragmented and unreliable.
  - Need for easy-to-understand digital safety guidance.

### Community Profile
- Local users include students, families, small-business owners, and senior citizens depending on digital transactions.
- The project is designed for users with mixed levels of digital literacy.
- Especially relevant for Telugu-speaking communities and first-time users.

---

## Slide 3 – Objectives

### Major Objectives
1. To create a multilingual platform that educates users on scam prevention and cyber safety.
2. To help users identify fake payment requests, phishing messages, and scam calls.
3. To provide guidance on safe UPI transactions and official reporting channels.
4. To make awareness materials available in simple, downloadable formats.
5. To support user-friendly learning with speech and accessibility features.

### Expected Outcomes
- Improved awareness of safe digital behavior.
- Better understanding of common scam patterns.
- Increased confidence for users handling online payments.
- Greater access to reliable awareness information in English and Telugu.

---

## Slide 4 – Problem Identification

### Community Needs
- Clear guidance on UPI safety and scam prevention.
- Awareness of fake links and fake QR code traps.
- Support for identifying job, loan, and investment scams.
- Simple access to reporting and help information.

### Challenges Identified
- Users often trust urgent messages or calls.
- Scam messages often mimic official communication.
- People may not know where or how to report suspicious activity.
- Many citizens do not have a single trusted digital-safety resource.

### Justification
- The project focuses on prevention and education, which is essential before fraud occurs.
- The solution is practical, low-cost, and scalable to the local community.

## Slide 5 – Methodology

### Data Collection Methods
- Review of project repository and awareness materials
- Review of local cyber-safety content and scam-awareness themes
- Analysis of common fraud patterns for UPI, phishing, fake offers, and digital arrest scams
- Browser-based testing of the portal and assistant workflow

### Tools and Techniques
- React + TypeScript + Vite
- Tailwind CSS for UI styling
- Express API for grounded help responses
- English-Telugu language switching
- Voice assistant using browser speech recognition and text-to-speech
- Downloadable awareness materials in project assets

### Workflow
1. User opens the portal.
2. Chooses language (English / Telugu).
3. Uses knowledge library or voice assistant for query support.
4. Reviews scam prevention guidance, checklists, and awareness content.
5. Downloads or shares safety materials.
